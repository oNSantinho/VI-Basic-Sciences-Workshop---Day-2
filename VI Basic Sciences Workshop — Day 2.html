Introduction

The VI Basic Sciences Workshop — Day 2 was a full-day academic event organized by UNAD, bringing together researchers, professors, and students to explore how foundational disciplines — Physics, Chemistry, Biology, and Mathematics — drive innovation across industry, education, and emerging technologies. Over 9 hours of live sessions, leading speakers presented cutting-edge applications from particle physics to renewable energy, highlighting the critical role of basic sciences in solving 21st-century challenges.

Conclusion

The VI Basic Sciences Workshop — Day 2 demonstrated that foundational sciences are the engine behind technological innovation, social progress, and scientific discovery. From the microscopic world of bubbles and subatomic particles to AI-powered systems and renewable energy, every session reinforced a single message: a strong grounding in Physics, Chemistry, Biology, and Mathematics is indispensable for solving the complex challenges of the 21st century. UNAD continues to position itself as a leading space where basic science and applied technology converge to build a better future.
